The toy training run

The paper's rank-1 B rotated around step 180.
B swings to its new direction over this many training steps. 10 = sudden turn; 80 = slow, gradual turn. Try 80: the dip almost vanishes, so a slow turn is hard to spot.
Real training is noisy: each step, B also moves a little in a random direction. More wobble = a bumpier cosine curve, and the lowest point can land in the wrong place. A bigger k helps.
